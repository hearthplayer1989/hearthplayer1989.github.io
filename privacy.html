HearthPlayer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

HearthPlayer is a free media player for smart TVs. It does not include any channels, films or other content. This policy explains what happens with your information when you use the app.

We do not collect your data

HearthPlayer has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. The developer does not receive, store or sell any personal information about you.

What is stored on your TV

To work, the app saves the following only on your TV, in the app's local storage:

  • the playlists you add (name, link, and for "Xtream Codes" playlists the server, username and password you entered);
  • your favourites, the position where you stopped a film or episode, the last channel you watched;
  • your settings (text size, contrast, subtitle language and size and similar) and, if you set one, your parental-control PIN;
  • if you sign in to OpenSubtitles.com in Settings: your OpenSubtitles username and the sign-in token it gives the app (not your password).

This information never leaves your TV except as described below. You can delete it at any time: delete a playlist in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

Connections the app makes

  • Your playlist provider. The app connects directly to the playlist and stream addresses that you entered, to load the channel list and play video. That provider receives your TV's IP address and, for Xtream Codes playlists, your login, as with any player. Their own privacy policy applies to them; HearthPlayer has no relationship with any provider.
  • OpenSubtitles.com (only when you use subtitle search). When you open the subtitle menu during a film or episode, the app sends the title (and, if your provider supplies them, the year, TMDB number, season and episode) and the language you chose to OpenSubtitles.com to find subtitles, and downloads the file you pick. If you sign in, your OpenSubtitles username and password are sent to OpenSubtitles.com to sign in. OpenSubtitles.com receives your TV's IP address; its own privacy policy applies. Nothing is sent if you do not open subtitle search.
  • Player libraries and font. When needed, the app loads the open-source playback libraries hls.js and mpegts.js from the jsDelivr network (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and its text font from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). These services receive your TV's IP address as part of a normal web request.

Content

HearthPlayer only plays content from playlists that you add. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to watch that content.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. A parental-control PIN is available in Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
