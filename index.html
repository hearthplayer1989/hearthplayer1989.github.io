HearthPlayer – a simple, easy-to-read TV player

HearthPlayer plays the M3U or Xtream Codes playlist you get from your own TV provider, with large text, clear colours and a classic TV guide.

HearthPlayer is a media player only. It does not include, sell or recommend any channels, films or playlists.
